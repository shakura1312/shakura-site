2018-2022

These are some samples of wood bowls that I produced on a lathe from 2018-now. They vary from walnut, to oak, to maple, to some experiements in plywood.

I liberated the plywood from abandoned projects in the OCADU studio and laminated it into blocks I could then turn.

I was very pleasantly suprised by how durable the plywood is

I enjoy this very old craft very much. It requires patience but also a strong stomach to jab big knives into a spinning piece of wood. I still have all my fingers !
