I worked as a landscape designer with DesignLand, a small landscape architecture studio in Toronto. The scope of work is primarily boutique atelier residential designs.

My role was contributing to concept development with sketches and rendering, and working on and completing construction documentation. I also did site and context research for projects and clients, from garden precedents to historical data and images. I worked to help create a stylistic direction with renderings that can be seen in the following images, as well as some nice lookin’ trees and stone samples that I like.
